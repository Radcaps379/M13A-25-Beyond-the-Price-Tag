# Recruitment Committee Brief — Lucas Stassin

**Attack · AS Saint-Étienne · France – Ligue 1 · age 20.1**

## Recommendation

Lucas Stassin is recommended for further investigation, not for an immediate approach. His model-implied value of EUR 31.1m is materially above his EUR 18.0m market value, while his quality percentile, development potential and value-efficiency scores make him attractive within the specified EUR 50m portfolio. However, the project's out-of-sample back-test did not support interpreting valuation gaps as exploitable undervaluation. The valuation discrepancy should therefore trigger investigation rather than confidence.

## Why the player merits consideration

Stassin ranks at the 90th percentile for quality. His development potential score is 0.762 and his value-efficiency score is 0.818. He played 1,868 league minutes during the season, providing a substantial performance sample for the valuation model.

## Performance and valuation evidence

His current market value is EUR 18.0m, while the model-implied value is EUR 31.1m, a valuation gap of 72.6%.

The strongest positive SHAP drivers are goal contributions per 90 (+1.028), age (+0.740), goals per 90 (+0.104), assists per 90 (+0.073) and league minutes played (+0.042).

The main negative drivers are playing in France – Ligue 1 (-0.372), minutes last season (-0.091), minutes per appearance (-0.045), contributions per 90 last season (-0.040) and three-season average minutes (-0.031).

These are explanations of the model's valuation, not proof that the market is incorrect. The model's held-out valuation performance was R² = 0.678, with median percentage error of 43.9%.

## Why the optimizer selected him

Stassin was selected under the EUR 50m scenario requiring one defender, one midfielder and one attacker. He combines 90th-percentile quality with development potential of 0.76 and value efficiency of 0.82, against a risk component of 0.12. His combination of quality, potential, affordability and comparatively low predicted exit risk makes him suitable for the illustrative portfolio.

## Key risks and uncertainties

His predicted probability of falling below 900 top-5 league minutes in the following season is 13.5%. The exit-risk model has a held-out AUC of 0.732.

His model-uncertainty score is 0.074. The principal analytical caution is the size of his valuation discrepancy: the project's back-test found no out-of-sample evidence that such gaps systematically translate into subsequent appreciation.

The model also has systematic residual differences across age groups and leagues, so the valuation gap should not be interpreted in isolation.

## What the model does not know

The system has no information on contract length and expiry, injury history or current fitness, scouting and tactical assessment, off-field circumstances or player willingness, or agent and release-clause situation.

The project found that players flagged by the valuation residual left top-five football at 3.7x the benchmark rate (30.1% versus 8.2%, p = 2.6e-11). This is a population-level finding, not a forecast that Stassin himself will leave.

The appropriate conclusion is therefore that the discrepancy warrants additional diligence, not that the player is definitively underpriced.

## Recommended human due diligence

1. Verify contract length, expiry and any release clause.
2. Review medical and fitness information that is not contained in the public analytical data.
3. Commission detailed scouting of his attacking contribution, role and tactical fit.
4. Establish the player's willingness to move and the selling club's expectations.
5. Investigate the source of the EUR 18.0m market value before treating the EUR 31.1m model estimate as an opportunity.
