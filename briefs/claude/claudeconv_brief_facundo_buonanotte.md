# Recruitment Committee Brief — Facundo Buonanotte

**Midfield · Leicester City · England – Premier League · age 20.0**

## Recommendation

Facundo Buonanotte is recommended for further investigation, not for an approach. His case differs from the other two recommendations in an important respect: the model and the market agree on his price almost exactly. He is put forward on quality and development grounds rather than on any apparent pricing discrepancy.

## Why the player merits consideration

Buonanotte sits in the 86th percentile for performance among midfielders. His development potential score is 0.767, the highest in the recommended portfolio. At 20.0 he has the longest remaining development runway of the three players.

## Performance and valuation evidence

Current market value is EUR 20.0m. The model-implied value is EUR 20.3m, a difference of 1.5%. Model and market are effectively aligned on this player.

He recorded 1,514 league minutes at 0.416 goal contributions per 90, the lowest minutes total of the three recommended players.

The factors raising the model's valuation are age (+0.764), goal contributions per 90 (+0.137) and league context (+0.344 in total across the league indicators, reflecting the Premier League premium).

The factors lowering it are league minutes played (-0.143), minutes per appearance (-0.099), club squad size and rotation (-0.082), disciplinary record (-0.055) and three-season average minutes (-0.051).

His minutes total and minutes per appearance both reduce the model's estimate. The reason for the limited minutes is not observable in the supplied data.

## Why the optimizer selected him

He was selected under a EUR 50m budget with one defender, one midfielder and one attacker required. He scored on quality at the 86th percentile, development potential 0.77, value efficiency 0.57, against a risk component of 0.24.

His value efficiency score of 0.567 is the weakest of the three recommendations. He was therefore not selected because he appears inexpensive relative to the model's estimate; he was selected on quality and development potential within the positional and budget constraints.

## Key risks and uncertainties

His predicted probability of falling below 900 top-5 league minutes next season is 24.9%, the highest in the recommended portfolio. This estimate comes from a model with a held-out AUC of 0.732. Given that he recorded 1,514 minutes this season, this is the most material risk attached to any of the three recommendations.

Model uncertainty for his segment scores 0.219, the lowest of the three, so the estimate itself is comparatively reliable. The valuation model nonetheless carries a median error of 43.9% on held-out data, and it systematically under-values players aged 21 and under, a pattern that replicates across three consecutive seasons.

## What the model does not know

The system observes match statistics, age, position, league and valuation history. It has no information on contract length or expiry, injury history or current fitness, scouting or tactical assessment, off-field circumstances, or agent and release-clause situation.

The system cannot observe why his minutes were limited. That question is central to assessing him and is not answerable from the supplied data.

The back-test finding remains relevant to how any recommendation from this system should be read: the hypothesis that model residuals identify exploitable undervaluation was tested out-of-sample and was not supported (p = 0.307).

## Recommended human due diligence

1. Establish why his league minutes were limited to 1,514. This is the single most important open question and the model cannot answer it.
2. Verify contract length, expiry and any release clause.
3. Obtain injury and fitness history from medical staff.
4. Commission live scouting on tactical fit and playing style.
5. Assess whether the 24.9% predicted exit risk is acceptable, and what would need to be true about his role for that risk to fall.
6. Note that no valuation discrepancy supports this recommendation. The case rests on quality and development potential, and should be evaluated on those terms.
