# Recruitment Committee Brief — Lucas Stassin

**Attack · AS Saint-Étienne · France – Ligue 1 · age 20.1**

## Recommendation

Lucas Stassin is recommended for further investigation. He is not recommended for an approach at this stage. The analytical system identifies him as a candidate on performance and development grounds, but it cannot establish that a transaction would represent value: its own out-of-sample back-test found no reliable relationship between model-implied valuation gaps and subsequent returns.

## Why the player merits consideration

Stassin sits in the 90th percentile for performance among players in his position, the highest quality score in the recommended portfolio. His development potential score is 0.762 and his value efficiency score is 0.818. At 20.1 he sits well inside the club's recruitment horizon.

## Performance and valuation evidence

Current market value is EUR 18.0m. The model-implied value is EUR 31.1m, a difference of 72.6%. Over the season he recorded 1,868 league minutes at 0.819 goal contributions per 90.

The factors raising the model's valuation are goal contributions per 90 (+1.028), age (+0.740), goals per 90 (+0.104), assists per 90 (+0.073) and minutes played (+0.042).

The factors lowering it are playing in France – Ligue 1 (-0.372), minutes last season (-0.091), minutes per appearance (-0.045), contributions per 90 last season (-0.040) and three-season average minutes (-0.031).

Attacking output is the dominant positive contributor to his valuation. The largest negative contribution is the league discount rather than any attribute of the player's own performance.

## Why the optimizer selected him

He was selected under a EUR 50m budget with one defender, one midfielder and one attacker required. He scored on quality at the 90th percentile, development potential 0.76, value efficiency 0.82, against a risk component of 0.12 — the lowest risk component in the portfolio.

## Key risks and uncertainties

His predicted probability of falling below 900 top-5 league minutes next season is 13.5%. This estimate comes from a model with a held-out AUC of 0.732. Model uncertainty for his segment scores 0.074, the lowest of the three recommended players.

The valuation model carries a median error of 43.9% on held-out data. A further limitation applies directly to a player of this age: the model systematically under-values players aged 21 and under and over-values players aged 28 and over, and this pattern replicates across three consecutive seasons. His model-implied value should be read with that bias in mind.

## What the model does not know

The system observes match statistics, age, position, league and valuation history. It has no information on contract length or expiry, injury history or current fitness, scouting or tactical assessment, off-field circumstances, or agent and release-clause situation.

The back-test result is material to how this brief should be read: the hypothesis that model residuals identify exploitable undervaluation was tested out-of-sample and was not supported (p = 0.307). Players flagged by residual left top-5 football at 3.7x the benchmark rate (30.1% versus 8.2%, p = 2.6e-11).

## Recommended human due diligence

1. Verify contract length, expiry and any release clause.
2. Obtain injury and fitness history from medical staff.
3. Commission live scouting on tactical fit and playing style.
4. Establish the selling club's position and the player's willingness to move.
5. Assess whether his output is sustainable outside Ligue 1, given that the league term is the largest negative contributor to the model's estimate.
6. Treat the 72.6% valuation gap as a question to investigate rather than as evidence of value.
